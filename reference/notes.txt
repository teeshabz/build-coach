The hook is the SAME in both cases. Only the fastening changes.
- Into a wood stud: the version with the pointed wood screw moulded into it. Nothing else.
- Into hollow drywall: the toggle bolt (metal wings on a long machine screw) OR the
  ribbed white plastic expansion anchor. One or the other, never both.

The two hooks look nearly identical on camera - same white curve. The only reliable
tell is what is attached to it: a pointed screw means the wood one, a metal toggle bar
or plastic sleeve means the drywall one. If neither is in frame, say so rather than guess.

Stud finder (red, JAXWQ, green LCD):
- Mode icon sits top-LEFT. An I-beam shape = METAL mode. A 3D box/cube = WOOD/STUD mode.
- A second box icon shows scan depth and varies (1/2", 1"). Changing depth is NOT a mode
  error and must not be corrected.
- The screen is GREEN at all times, idle and scanning alike. Green never means "found it".
- While sweeping it shows a bar graph and a small sideways arrow. That arrow means keep moving
  that way - it has NOT found the centre yet. Do not call a stud off a sideways arrow.
- It has FOUND the centre only when the word CENTER appears in a dark bar at the bottom of the
  screen, with a big filled arrow pointing up and the bars solid. That word is the only
  reliable 'found it' signal.
